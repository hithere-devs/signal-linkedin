# Chrome Web Store listing

## Product name

Signal: LinkedIn Feed Intelligence

## Summary

Score LinkedIn posts for relevance, filter noise and ads, and keep control of what reaches your feed.

## Category

Productivity

## Language

English

## Detailed description

Signal turns your LinkedIn feed into a relevance-ranked inbox.

Tell Signal about your role, skills, interests, career goals, target companies, and topics you would rather avoid. It scores visible posts from 0 to 100, explains the result, and applies the filtering mode you choose.

WHAT YOU CONTROL

• Choose any score threshold from 0 to 100
• Collapse, hide, blur, or only score low-value posts
• Remove sponsored content
• Always show or hide selected posts
• Give direct useful or not-useful feedback
• Review seven days of aggregate feed statistics

LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Signal's built-in heuristic engine runs in your browser and works without an account. There are no ads, analytics, tracking pixels, or remote executable code.

OPTIONAL FEATURES

Create an account to sync your profile, preferences, feedback, overrides, and recent aggregate statistics across browsers. AI keys and cached post text are never synced.

For deeper analysis of ambiguous posts, connect an OpenAI-compatible provider, Groq, OpenRouter, Gemini, Ollama, or LM Studio. Signal requests access to that host only when you enable it. Images are shared only when Vision is enabled separately.

OPEN SOURCE

https://github.com/hithere-devs/signal-linkedin
