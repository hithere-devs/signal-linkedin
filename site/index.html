Scores you can understand.

Signal adds scores to the LinkedIn feed you already use.

Try the score badges

Start with what matters to you

Your role, interests, skills, and goals guide the score. Add people to prioritize and topics you'd rather skip.

Explore the profile editor

Choose what happens to the rest

Collapse, hide, blur, or just show scores. Adjust your threshold, remove sponsored posts, and pause whenever you want.

Try the filter controls

Understand every decision

Open a score badge to see the reasons. Give feedback when Signal gets it wrong. Your choices help shape future scores.

Local scoring comes standard.

Scoring works on your device without an account. AI and cloud sync are optional. No analytics, ads, or tracking pixels.

Make it part
of your browser.

Signal is a Chrome extension, available as an open-source build while the Chrome Web Store listing is pending.

  1. Download the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the unzipped extension folder.
