Privacy Policy

Signal is a Chrome extension that scores and filters posts on LinkedIn. This policy explains what it handles and where that data goes.

Local processing

By default, Signal processes visible LinkedIn post text, author details, hashtags, and media metadata locally. Your professional profile, preferences, feedback, aggregate statistics, and cached scores live in Chrome extension storage.

Signal has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or remote executable code.

Optional cloud sync

If you create an account, Supabase authenticates you and stores your profile, preferences, feedback signals, per-post overrides, and up to 30 days of aggregate statistics. Row-level access controls restrict each record to its user.

AI keys, cached post text, and AI responses are never included in cloud sync. Authentication tokens remain in Chrome extension storage so your session can persist.

Optional AI providers

If you enable AI analysis, Signal sends selected post text and metadata to the provider you configure. If you separately enable Vision, Signal may send up to two post images. The provider's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Retention and control

Local data remains until you remove the extension or choose Delete local data. Synced data remains until you choose Delete account. Settings also lets you export your non-secret data.

Permissions

  • Storage: saves extension settings and data.
  • Alarms: schedules delayed and periodic account sync.
  • LinkedIn access: reads visible feed posts and adds filtering controls.
  • Optional site access: contacts only a cloud or AI host you approve. LinkedIn CDN access is requested only for Vision.

Contact

Open a privacy question at GitHub Issues. Material changes will be documented in the repository and reflected by a new effective date.
