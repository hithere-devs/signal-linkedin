# Product Hunt launch kit

## Name

Signal: LinkedIn Feed Intelligence

## Tagline

Turn your LinkedIn feed into a relevance-ranked inbox

## Short description

Signal scores every visible LinkedIn post against your goals, explains the result, and filters low-value content. It works locally by default, with optional account sync and bring-your-own AI.

## Maker comment

I built Signal because my LinkedIn feed kept optimizing for reaction, not usefulness.

Signal gives every visible post a 0 to 100 score based on the profile you control. Low-value posts can be collapsed, hidden, or blurred. Every score is explainable, and direct feedback improves future decisions.

The local scorer works without an account or API key. Cloud sync and AI are optional. The code is open source, there is no analytics, and AI credentials never enter the sync payload.

I would value feedback on three things: whether the default threshold feels right, which posts Signal misclassifies, and what would make the explanations more useful.
