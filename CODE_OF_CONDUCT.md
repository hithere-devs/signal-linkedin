# Code of Conduct

We want Signal's community to be useful, respectful, and safe.

Be considerate. Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized language or imagery. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct harms the community.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private security contact or directly to a maintainer. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where possible.
